**New in 0.10**

- **Tap a picture to see it full screen.** On a note, the picture is shown whole instead of cropped, and a tap opens it full screen. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag to look around, and swipe down or press Back to return. **Open in another app** hands it to your gallery.
- **Hear about new versions.** Engram can tell you when a newer version is out. If you installed it from Google Play, it asks Play and updates through Play's own screen. Otherwise it looks at the releases page on GitHub and shows what changed. It asks first, checks at most once a day, and sends nothing of yours. Change it in Settings, Help and about, **New versions**, where **Check now** works any time.
- **All the text read from a picture.** The "Text in this picture" panel showed only 12 lines. It now shows everything, joins lines the picture wrapped into proper paragraphs, and reads long screenshots in pieces so small print isn't lost. Pictures read before keep their old text until you tap **Read it again**.

**Fixed in 0.10**

- Google Play no longer limits Engram to phones with Wi-Fi and a portrait screen, so tablets and other devices can install it.

**Please try these on your phone**

- Open a note with a photo: tap it, zoom with two fingers and with a double tap, then swipe down.
- Share a long screenshot to Engram and check the text under it against the picture.
- Settings, Help and about, New versions: tap **Check now**. On this version it should say you have the newest one.

**Install**

Download the APK below on an Android phone (Android 7 or newer, 64-bit) and open it. It installs over 0.9.

**Coming from 0.7 or 0.8? One reinstall is needed.** Since 0.9, Engram is signed with its own permanent key, and Android won't install it over the old test-key builds. Back up in your current Engram (Settings, Your data, **Back up**), uninstall it, install this APK, then **Restore** the zip.

**Known limits**

- People on 0.9 won't get a notice for this version, because the notice arrives with it. From 0.10 on, it tells you.
- The weekly automatic backup still holds words, tags and links only, not photos or recordings.
- A backup zip is built in memory; with more than about 400 MB of media, the largest files are left out and the count is shown.
