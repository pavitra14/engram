This is a **beta** for testing. Expect rough edges, and please report what breaks.

**Install:** download the APK below on an Android phone (Android 7 or newer, 64-bit) and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your thoughts.

**Fixed in 0.5**

- **Voice notes work again.** Since 0.2 the app had not been declaring the microphone permission, so Android never offered it and recording said the microphone was turned off. The first time you tap the mic you should now be asked.

**New in 0.5**

- **Translate into English** (Settings, Translation; off until you turn it on). Notes in other languages get an English version under them, made on your phone. The original is kept. The first note in each language downloads a language pack of about 30 MB.
- **A question a day** on Today. Answer it and it becomes a note. "Not today" hides it; Settings switches it off.
- **Your week.** Tap "This week" on Today: notes, to-dos done, your streak, and a few older notes to keep, turn into to-dos or let go.
- **Templates** for a new note: Idea, Meeting, Book or article, Decision, Day.
- **Engram Cloud is switched off for now.** It is hidden behind a feature flag until it has been tested, so the Settings section from 0.4 is gone. If you signed in on 0.4, syncing has stopped; nothing on your phone is affected.

**Please try these on your phone; they could not be tested automatically**

- Tap the mic: are you asked for the microphone, and does recording and transcription work?
- Turn on translation and write or speak a note in Hindi or another language.
- The question, the weekly review, and starting a note from a template.

**Known limits**

- Translation quality is casual, and formatting is not carried into the English version.
- Group names are single words and can be odd; groups cannot be renamed.
- Backups do not include files or recordings.
- Signed with a development key: fine for installing directly, not for the Play Store.
