This is a **beta** for testing. Expect rough edges, and please report what breaks.

**Install:** download the APK below on an Android phone (Android 7 or newer, 64-bit) and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your thoughts.

**Fixed in 0.6**

- **Translation no longer blames a "language pack" for every failure.** Turning it on used to stop at the first note that failed and always said a pack could not be downloaded. Now each note is tried on its own, English notes are never sent to the translator, and a failure shows what the phone actually reported. Settings has "Try again" for the notes that were skipped.

**New in 0.6: catching things**

- **Reminders from plain words.** "Call the bank Friday 10am" or "renew passport in 2 weeks", typed or spoken, sets the reminder. A message offers Undo. Switch off in Settings.
- **Quick Settings tile** ("New thought") and **shortcuts** on the app icon (hold it): voice note, new note, to-dos.
- **Shake to record**, while Engram is open (off until you turn it on).
- **Quick-note notification** that stays in the shade (off until you turn it on).
- **Add to another note** when sharing in, instead of always making a new one.
- **Scan paper or a whiteboard** from the plus button: the picture is kept and its words become the note.
- **Text in pictures is searchable.** Read on the phone; Latin-script text only.

**Finding and linking**

- **Pin** notes to the top of Today. **`[[Title]]`** in a note links it to the note with that title. **`@name`** files a note under that person in Search. **Saved searches.**
- **"These two seem related"**: Engram asks about pairs it was unsure of. Your answer is remembered either way.
- **Rename a group, merge two groups**, and give a group a **goal and a date**: it then shows progress from the check boxes in its notes.
- **A warning when you write something you already wrote.**

**Coming back to things**

- **Remember this** (hold a note): it returns after 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days. **Decisions** made from the template come back after three months.
- **On this day**, and **Surprise me**, on Today. A third widget, **From your notes**.
- **The month in review**, and **play the map** growing from your first thought.
- Quiet **milestones**.

**Getting things done**

- **Check boxes in any note appear in To do** and can be ticked there.
- **Focus for 25 minutes** on a to-do. **Waiting on someone** and **Someday** lists. **Habits** with a count of days in a row.
- **Morning brief** and **evening wrap-up** notifications (both off until you turn them on).

**Yours**

- **Trash**: deleting moves a note to the trash for 30 days, with Undo.
- **App lock** with fingerprint, face or PIN.
- **Accent colour.** **Weekly backup** to a folder you choose. **Export as Markdown.** **Bring in notes** from Markdown, text or Google Keep.

**Not built, and why**

- Wear OS tile (needs a separate watch app), asking your notes questions with an on-phone language model (a download of about 1 GB that cannot be tested here), reminders by place (needs location in the background), password-protected backups, and sharing a note as a picture. Double-tap on the back of the phone cannot be claimed by an app; on a Pixel, point Quick Tap at Engram.

**Please try these on your phone; none could be tested automatically**

- Translation in a language other than English: does it work now, and if not, what does the message say?
- The Quick Settings tile, the icon shortcuts, shake, and the quick-note notification.
- Scan a page; search for a word in a photo.
- App lock on and off. The focus timer running out. Morning and evening notifications.
- Weekly backup to a folder, Markdown export, and importing notes.

**Known limits**

- Reading text from pictures needs Google Play services and does not read Hindi or other non-Latin scripts.
- The trash, pins, parked lists, group names and goals stay on this phone; Engram Cloud (still switched off) does not carry them.
- The morning brief is as fresh as the last time Engram was open.
- Backups do not include files or recordings.
- Signed with a development key: fine for installing directly, not for the Play Store.
